# Dependency and asset notices

The opt-in Hey VYBE listener uses Vosk Android 0.3.75 and the unmodified
`vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15` English model under Apache-2.0. JNA 5.18.1 is
used under its Apache-2.0 option. Bundled license and provenance:
`app/src/main/assets/licenses/Vosk-Apache-2.0.txt` and `Vosk-NOTICE.txt`.
Model source: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models . The approximately 40 MB
model is bundled for offline speech recognition; recognized song-search text
is sent to YouTube. No microphone audio is saved or uploaded by this listener.
Synthetic Windows David speech WAVs in androidTest assets are test fixtures,
not user microphone recordings, and are excluded from the production APK.

Muse uses original name treatment, equalizer launcher glyph, geometric artwork and deterministic synthesized PCM samples generated by `tools/generate-original-media.py`. No Spotify artwork/logo or third-party music recording is bundled. The generated audio is test media, not a YouTube download.
